Just ask

Say it the way you would to a person.

No special words, no settings to learn. Here is what happens with three everyday asks.

You"Build a website for my business."

What I do

  • Ask a few quick questions: what you sell, who it is for, the look you like.
  • Write the words and build the pages.
  • Show you the finished site to look over and change.
  • Put it online only when you say so.

You"Fit my work schedule around the kids' basketball games and put them on the calendar."

What I do

  • Read the game schedule and your work hours.
  • Spot the clashes and suggest a week that works.
  • Lay out the calendar changes for you to check.
  • Add them once you OK the plan.

You"Send Missy the reservation details."

What I do

  • Find the reservation: the place, the time, the confirmation.
  • Write a short note in your voice.
  • Show you exactly what will be sent.
  • Send it after you say yes.

How it works

Three steps. That is the whole manual.

Behind the scenes I run a small team of AIs and pick the right one for each part of the job. You never have to manage them.

  1. 1

    Tell me

    Type it on your computer or your iPhone. Plain words are enough.

  2. 2

    I get to work

    I plan it, hand each part to the AI best suited for it, and keep you posted as it moves. On iPhone, progress shows in the Dynamic Island.

  3. 3

    You approve

    You see the result before anything goes out. One tap to approve or stop.
